01 / How to play

Two ways to play.
So many reasons to doubt.

01 / No-word mode?

No word. Keep your cool.

Everyone else knows the secret word. The imposter has to piece it together from their clues. Sounding too confident might give you away.

02 / Two-word mode≠

Same conversation. Different words.

Players receive similar words and see only their own word, never their team. Listen carefully: are you really talking about the same thing?

02 / HINTVEIL

Pass the phone.
Let the suspicion begin.

  1. 01

    Get your people together

    Choose your player count, mode and deck. 3–12 people, one iPhone, no account required.

  2. 02

    Keep your card to yourself

    Take turns holding to peek. Release to hide, then pass the phone. Keep the secret in the game.

  3. 03

    Give clues. Discuss. Vote.

    Describe your word and listen for what doesn’t fit. Vote together; the host records who is out. Keep going until a team wins.

03 / AI

A college reunion, a team get-together, or a topic only your friends would pick. Give AI a theme and get a deck for your table. You bring the people. They bring the suspicion.
